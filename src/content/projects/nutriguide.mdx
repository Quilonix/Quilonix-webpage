# Overview

In a world filled with overly complex health trackers, **NutriGuide** takes a radically different approach to wellness. Built with absolute simplicity and elegance in mind, it is designed to help you build healthy habits consistently—without the pressure of overwhelming metrics.

Instead of confusing you with endless numbers, calorie counting, and rigid goals, NutriGuide focuses on what truly matters for long-term health:
- **Daily Actions:** Encouraging small, consistent positive choices.
- **Balance:** Ensuring a sustainable approach to well-being.
- **Long-term Progress:** Celebrating the journey rather than just the destination.

## Engineering & Architecture

NutriGuide was developed as a cross-platform mobile application, prioritizing a fluid, native-like user experience.

### Tech Stack
- **Framework:** Flutter
- **Design Philosophy:** Minimalist UI/UX for frictionless daily interaction
- **Deployment:** Google Play Store

By leveraging **Flutter**, we were able to deliver a buttery-smooth interface with custom animations that make tracking habits feel like a reward rather than a chore. The architecture is built to be lightweight, ensuring fast load times and minimal battery consumption for our users.

## Why We Built It

At Quilonix, we believe technology should empower people, not cause anxiety. We noticed that most health apps on the market create "notification fatigue" and stress when users miss a target. NutriGuide was engineered to be the antidote—a supportive, elegant tool that fits seamlessly into a balanced lifestyle.

You can download and experience NutriGuide today on the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nextgenx.nutriguid_app).
