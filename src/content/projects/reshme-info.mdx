# Overview

**Reshme Info** is a mission-driven mobile application built specifically to empower the agricultural community—specifically farmers who specialize in **sericulture** (the rearing of silkworms for the production of silk).

Agriculture in niche sectors often suffers from a lack of accessible, localized, and modern digital tooling. Reshme Info bridges this gap by bringing crucial information, best practices, and farming guidelines directly to the farmer's smartphone.

## The Impact

Sericulture requires precise environmental control, timing, and specific knowledge to ensure a healthy yield. A single mistake in the rearing process can compromise an entire cycle. 

Through this application, farmers are equipped with:
- **Expert Guidelines:** Step-by-step methodologies for optimal silkworm rearing.
- **Accessible UI:** A straightforward interface designed for users who may not be highly tech-savvy, ensuring the technology is inclusive.
- **Real-time Resource Access:** Vital information available at their fingertips, right in the field.

## Engineering

Building for rural and agricultural sectors requires a unique approach to software engineering. The application needed to be highly reliable, easy to navigate, and optimized for various network conditions.

- **Platform:** Android (Google Play Store)
- **Focus:** High accessibility, low-latency data access, and intuitive UX/UI flow.
